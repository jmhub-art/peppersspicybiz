The Kathi Pepper Guide: Anatomy of a Pepper's Party

Step 1: Schedule the Party

Start by getting your hostess' contact details: address, email, and phone number. Confirm a date & time that works for her and her guests. Remember, parties are often booked at other parties!

💡 Tip: Give her plenty of your business cards! You may also receive referrals directly from Pepper’s HQ. Make sure you're set up with a central hub to track party leads, hostess info, and guests — something like Atrex or another CRM. At Pepper’s Parties, we use Brevo and SendFox for email communications and the WooCommerce/Atrex interface for customer and order management. Modern tools can make all the difference:

  • CRM or POS: Use Atrex or similar to keep your parties, inventory, and follow-ups organized.
  • Email Services: Brevo (for automation) and SendFox (for newsletters) are great tools to stay connected.
  • Social/Group Tools: Facebook Groups and Messenger chats make it easy to keep party buzz alive and encourage engagement.

💡 Tip: By using the notes section in Atrex, you can document every call or message with a time-stamped entry. This keeps your hostess follow-ups organized and professional — plus, it shows you’re running a classy, tight ship!

Phone calls are essential in this business. We're in a human, heart-centered industry — and that friendly voice goes a long way in creating connection, trust, and excitement. That said, today’s hostesses love convenience too, so it’s great to:

  • Set up a Facebook group for the party
  • 💡 Tip: For example, PeppersPartiesToo.net gives each hostess her own landing page to track sales, earn credits, and redeem discounts or toys. This not only boosts engagement — it turns her into your brand ambassador for years to come!
  • Use group chats or Messenger for quick updates
  • Send text reminders with flair (think: “Spicy Show Countdown: 3 days!”)

Help her succeed — many hostesses are first-timers! Keep her engaged with a few follow-up calls:

  • Call 2 days after booking to confirm.
  • Deliver or send her hostess packet: letter, cards, order forms, invites, and a balloon.
  • Call again to confirm packet received, answer Qs, give directions, explain order room & benefits.
  • Day-of reminder call — keep the energy high!

If she cancels: stay sweet & professional. Reschedule if possible.

Step 2: Plan Your Inventory

Estimate guest count — more guests = more goodies! Be sure to ask the hostess if she has received the Flip Book Catalog and shared it with her guests. This gets the conversation started early and builds excitement. Ask if there are any specific items that guests are already buzzing about — that way, you can tailor your kit for extra sizzle!

Make sure you're using the current 2025 Kathi Order Form for your show sales — it aligns with the latest product offerings listed on Pepper’s Parties Online Boutique. Having the most current info means smoother ordering and bigger sales!

Be sure to stock top sellers like:

  • Cutie Wand
  • Sensuelle Panty Bullet
  • JO Flavored Lube
  • Pure Instinct Entice

💡 Kit rule: The bigger your kit, the better your sales!

Step 3: Pre-Party Checklist

  • Fully stocked kit & table (or check if hostess has one)
  • Brochures, biz cards, order forms (in folders)
  • POS/Computer/Calculator
  • Maps/directions & hostess contact
  • Special request items
  • Cash change: $20s, $5s, $1s, coins
  • Appointment book
  • Game prizes, pens, bags

Step 4: Arrive at the Party

Show up early and friendly. Set the vibe!

  • Intro yourself warmly. Let the hostess help carry things in if she offers.
  • Set up demo room in semi-circle seating.
  • Choose a private room for orders (bedrooms work great).
  • Organize your products in presentation order — easier = smoother.

Step 5: Make the Presentation

Welcome guests with warmth, confidence, and a flirty smile. Then:

  • Introduce yourself: “Hi y’all, I’m Kathi Pepper and I’m your guide to tonight’s spicy fun!”
  • Play a quick name game or icebreaker.
  • Explain how the party will flow and the private ordering process.
  • Start with lighter products — lotions, games, massage.
  • Break Time: Let them snack while you set up the bedroom line.
  • Optional: Play the Peter Game for laughs and easing nerves!
  • Finish strong — thank everyone, especially your hostess!

Final Notes

Always maintain a positive, professional tone — even if things go sideways. Your energy sets the room!

✨ Keep the party about education, empowerment, and a whole lotta fun. That’s the Pepper’s Promise.
