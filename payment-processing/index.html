Understanding "High-Risk" Classification

Adult products are classified as "high-risk" by payment processors due to several factors:

  • Higher chargeback rates: Includes "friendly fraud" where customers dispute charges when partners discover purchases
  • Complex identity verification: Age verification requirements add compliance layers
  • Regulatory scrutiny: Additional oversight and legal requirements
  • Reputational concerns: Some financial institutions avoid adult product businesses
💡 What This Means for You:
Higher processing fees (typically 3-5% more than standard), potential registration fees, stricter underwriting, and possible reserve requirements. Understanding these challenges upfront helps you make informed decisions.
